## 3. Long-Form Narrative (Press Kit / Website)

### Game Overview

"Eggs are falling fast. Protect your cannon — or it's game over."

Bird Hunter isn't your average mobile game. The sky above you is alive — birds are closing in, eggs are dropping fast, and the chaos keeps building with every second you wait. You have a cannon. You have one job. Blast as many eggs as you can before they hit the ground and damage your cannon. Simple to understand, brutally hard to master — and impossible to put down.

### The Hook

Most games ask you to survive. **Bird Hunter dares you to stay in control.**

The arena is yours — until the birds show up. They come in waves, they come in numbers, and they come with one goal: overwhelm you. As they swoop across the sky, they drop eggs that fall straight toward the ground. Your job is to shoot them down before they break through your defense. Miss too many and the pressure builds. Every egg that slips through damages your cannon — and if it takes too many hits, it won't survive.

What makes it electric is what happens when you *hit*. Bigger eggs don't just disappear — they crack open and split into smaller ones, scattering across the screen and multiplying the chaos in an instant. One well-placed shot can set off a chain reaction that clears the board. A badly-timed one can bury you under a storm of shells you never saw coming.

This is where skill separates the good players from the great ones.

### Core Gameplay

**Simple to pick up. Brutal to master.**

You control a cannon at the bottom of the screen. Drag left or right to line up your shot, fire upward to destroy falling eggs. Before each round, you pick which cannon you want to use — and that choice shapes everything about how the level plays out.

**The Egg Tier System** — Eggs come in four sizes (E1 through E4). Destroy a large egg and it splits into two medium eggs. Destroy those and you get four small ones. Massive eggs cascade further. One bullet can eventually become eight falling targets. Good players learn to work top-down: handle the big ones first before the chain floods the screen.

**The Pressure System** — Every egg carries a weight value. When the total on-screen weight crosses the level's threshold, birds temporarily stop releasing new eggs. It's a brief window to breathe. If you're not clearing fast enough, the moment it lifts, the screen floods again. The game keeps roughly 10–15 eggs in play at any time — chaotic, but manageable if you stay sharp.

**Combos & Scoring** — Chain hits together to build a combo multiplier that boosts your score and drops bonus coins. Accuracy isn't just satisfying — it's mechanically rewarded.

**Win condition** — Hit the target score before time runs out. Every egg that reaches the ground damages your cannon. Let too many through and the run ends, regardless of score or remaining time. Close to failing? Trade a short ad for extra time. Whether that's worth it is your call.

### Difficulty Curve

Difficulty climbs two ways at once. Across **30 chapters**, birds become more complex, eggs carry higher pressure, and score targets sharpen. Within each chapter's **20 levels**, difficulty builds progressively with spikes at key levels designed to align with reward moments.

When the game detects sustained struggle, an **adaptive relief system** temporarily eases spawn rates and egg durability. Once your performance stabilises, the intended progression returns.

### Cannons & Customization

Seven cannons. Seven identities. The choice isn't cosmetic — it reshapes how every level feels.

- **Single Shot** — the balanced baseline. Steady, predictable, the cannon you measure all others against.
- **Rapid Fire** — high firing speed, sustained pressure. Built for relentless waves.
- **Shotgun Spread** — wide bullet cones that cover ground a focused shot never could.
- **Big Bertha** — explosive impact damage. Clears clusters in a single hit.
- **Lucky** — landing critical strikes that punch through tough egg tiers.
- **Double** — two streams of fire. Twice the chaos.
- **Triple** — three-shot pattern. Maximum firepower in the right hands.

Each cannon's bullet behaviour is baked into its identity — switching between levels isn't about preference, it's about reading the next wave and preparing accordingly.

### Upgrade System

Every cannon has **10 upgrade tiers**. The difference between a tier-1 cannon and a fully-upgraded one isn't subtle — it's the gap between surviving a chapter and dominating it.

Upgrades cost **coins** (earned through gameplay, combos, and clears) and **materials** (earned through milestones and spin rewards). Neither is handed to you. Damage output, fire rate, and special scaling all improve — but each cannon has its own upgrade curve, so investment paths feel distinct.

No single cannon outperforms everything at every tier. You're not grinding toward one best option — you're building a toolkit and learning when to use each piece.

### Power-Up Spins

Every few levels, you earn a **slot-machine spin**. Every chapter starts with one (slot 0), and you unlock three more at levels 5, 10, and 15. Each spin offers three options drawn from a pool of **29 power-ups across four rarities** — Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Build a loadout that fits your cannon and the chapter ahead. No two runs play the same.

### Live-Service Features

- **Daily tasks** that refresh and reward consistent play
- **Achievements** tracking long-term mastery
- **Global leaderboards** powered by Unity Services
- **Cloud save** that follows you across devices (Google Play Games on Android, Game Center on iOS, anonymous fallback)
- **Boss encounters** that punctuate progression
- **Optional rewarded ads** for revive — never forced, always your call
